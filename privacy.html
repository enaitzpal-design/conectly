Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

Conectly is a student project by a team of entrepreneurship students (LEINN, Mondragon University) in Berlin. It is not a company yet. We are testing whether founders want live video calls with founders in other countries. Contact: conectly.team@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • What you type in our sign-up form: email, name, nationality, company or project, and your answers to the optional questions.
  • Where you came from (for example a flyer or an ad), so we know which channel works.
  • If you allow it, a Meta pixel on our thank-you page tells Meta that someone signed up from an ad. It does not send your answers.

Why

  • To contact you and arrange your free call with another founder.
  • To learn from the answers for our university research. Results are only shared in anonymous, combined form.
  • To measure which ads bring sign-ups (only with your consent).

Legal basis: your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by writing to us.

Who processes it

  • Tally (Tally BV, Belgium) hosts the form and stores your answers in the EU.
  • Meta Platforms Ireland receives the sign-up event from the pixel, only if you consented.

We never sell your data and we don't share it with anyone else, except the founder you are matched with, who only sees your name and what you told us about your company.

How long

We delete your data when the project ends, at the latest on 31 March 2027, or earlier if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its use, by emailing conectly.team@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Berlin: the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).
